MEV Dojo

You're an MEV searcher. Reorder the block. Take the profit.

Every block, ordinary users broadcast trades into a public mempool. As the searcher, you decide the order those trades run — and you can slip your own trades in between. Order them right and you skim profit from their price impact. That's MEV — Maximal Extractable Value.

The three areas

Price chart (top) — the token price as the block runs, left → right. It lines up exactly with the boxes below.
Your block (middle) — drag boxes here. Left-to-right = execution order.
Public mempool (bottom) — the victims' pending trades. Drag them up into your block.

Reading a box

▲ Green = BUY pushes the price up. ▼ Red = SELL pushes the price down. Boxes with a cyan glow are yours; muted boxes are victims.

How to play

  1. 1Drag the victims' trades from the mempool up into your block.
  2. 2Drag Buy / Sell templates from the left to insert your own trades. Use the slider on a box to size it.
  3. 3Order everything. The classic move — a sandwich: buy right before a victim's buy, then sell right after. You bought cheap, their trade pushed the price up, you sold high.
  4. 4Hit Simulate Block to watch it execute and see who lost what.

Winning

Beat the level's profit target — and end holding only USDC. Any leftover token means you're exposed to the next block's price move, so unwind back to your starting bag to lock the profit in.

Miss an opportunity and a rival bot may grab it first. Use Prev / Next (bottom-left) to move between levels.
